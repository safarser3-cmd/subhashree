Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 1, 2026

Welcome to the Subhashree Sahu Official Fan Community ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy governs the privacy policies and practices of our website, located at subhaslyf.vercel.app.

1. Information We Collect

When you use our application, particularly when you log in using your Google account to submit Fan Art, we collect the following personal information:

  • Email Address: We collect your email address provided by Google OAuth. This is used solely to uniquely identify you, associate your uploaded Fan Art with your account, and manage your submissions.
  • Name and Profile Picture: We may collect your name and profile picture to display alongside your community posts, comments, or artwork submissions if you choose to interact publicly on our platform.
  • User-Generated Content: Any fan art, comments, or messages you submit to the platform are stored on our servers (Firebase / Cloudflare) to display them to the community.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect primarily to provide, maintain, and improve our services. Specifically, we use your data to:

  • Authenticate your login sessions via Google OAuth.
  • Attribute your Fan Art submissions and comments to you.
  • Provide administrative features, such as allowing you to delete your own submissions.
  • Prevent abuse, spam, and malicious activity on the platform.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Your data is only shared with trusted third-party service providers (such as Google Firebase for database/authentication hosting and Vercel for web hosting) strictly for the purpose of operating the website.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your personal information only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy. You have the right to request the deletion of your data and any Fan Art or content you have submitted. To request data deletion, please contact the site administrator.

5. Third-Party Authentication (Google OAuth)

Our website utilizes Google OAuth for secure login. By logging in with Google, you agree to allow us to access your basic profile information (Name, Email, Profile Picture) as granted by your Google privacy settings. We do not have access to your Google password or any other private Google data.

6. Security of Your Data

We implement strict security rules using Firebase Authentication and Firestore Security Rules to ensure that your email address and personal data are protected against unauthorized access.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact the community administrator.
